# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community. Participation must remain free from harassment, intimidation, discrimination, and personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give actionable, respectful review feedback.
- Protect private and security-sensitive information.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Respect project scope and maintainer decisions while allowing constructive disagreement.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberate disruption or repeated bad-faith engagement.
- Retaliation against people reporting concerns.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants based on severity and pattern.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Maintainers involved in a complaint must recuse themselves from deciding its outcome.
